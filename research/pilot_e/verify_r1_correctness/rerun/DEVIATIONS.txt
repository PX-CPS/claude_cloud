A. Deviations = bug fixes caught by unit tests (fixed before any kill statistic was inspected)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1 [2026-09-29T13:20Z] U5 (semantic check) failed (rel. err 2.4) for the P05 output-referencing
   representation: wall springs were counted as two-body "pair" interactions, so P05 (walls) was
   treated as isolated and got the PC-style last-output reference.  Fix: corpus.is_pair() excludes
   walls / pivots / fixed centres.  This also fixes #pair counts used in section 7.1 (P05: 1 pair).
A2 [2026-09-29T13:10Z] Family-P ground truth: sp.simplify on the 5-body sums did not finish in 10 min.
   The spec requires sp.simplify only for family G; family-P truth is sp.expand(F/m) and is
   cross-checked numerically against Euler-Lagrange (3 random points, rel. tol 1e-10) for every
   conservative family-P system.

A3 [2026-09-29T13:55Z] U5 on miner-rewritten C4 representations raised KeyError: the numeric
   decoder evaluated template bodies in an environment containing only the holes, so a corpus
   dictionary constant ('d', i) used inside a miner template could not be resolved.  Fix (decoder
   only; no code length changes): template bodies are evaluated in the caller's global environment
   extended by the hole bindings.  Caught in the first full run before any statistic was computed.

B. Deviation outside the corpus (scaling series, used only by the conditional test 7.3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B1 N-link pendulum N=4 (SNL4): the symbolic 4x4 inverse (LU + cancel) did not finish in >20 min, so
   the explicit-EOM candidate of the fair min is omitted for SNL4 (it is dominated by the implicit
   form, as it already is for N=3), and its numeric ground truth solves M(q) z = f numerically.
   The formulation count (flag bits) still counts the explicit formulation as licensed.

C. Implementation choices where the frozen spec is silent or ambiguous (fixed before any run)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C1  Symbols are global: identical names denote the same entity (literal reading of section 2);
    P06 and P11 therefore share bodies m1..m4.  Unnamed bodies are mA, mB, mC (P08, P12-P15).
    Family-G symbols (except g) are distinct sympy objects (real=True) with the listed names.
C2  Machine levels: the implicit (linear-solve) formulation is offered to family G at D1/D2 (spec 1.4
    text); family P uses the Newtonian formulation, plus at D2 the gradient form -Dq(Vtmp,x_i)/m_i
    (+ non-conservative terms) as an alternative program for the same (Newtonian) decoder.
    Family G at D2: implicit residuals may also be written Dt(Dq(Ltmp,v_i)) - Dq(Ltmp,q_i).
C3  Formulations (flag = log2 #licensed): P {newton, el}; G {explicit, implicit (D1,D2), el}.
    Output referencing (a_N = -sum m_i O_i / m_N, or -sum O_i under N2) and the gradient form are
    free alternatives inside the Newtonian formulation (same decoder, no flag).
C4  Explicit per-body 2D force components are merged into one V(sum_x, sum_y); template calls, Cn(...)
    and N3 pair temps remain separate summands.  Per-term template-vs-explicit choice uses the
    standalone code length of the term (as scratch).
C5  Scope (C1 leaf = log2|scope|): item j (temps first, then outputs) sees state + params + all items
    before it.  Implicit forms add the n acceleration symbols to the state scope.
C6  Greedy inlining uses the strict inequality of the spec ("exceed").
C7  Law statements: each item pays Elias-gamma(#formals+1) plus its tree with leaves = formals;
    schema operators (Sm, Cr, Dt, Dv, Dq) are Calls with |lib(T)|+5 (scratch); C4 statements are coded
    as C1; C3 statements = text + 3 chars per item.  HK pays the templates actually used in the corpus
    under T (Fh1 if none, as scratch); UG pays Fg and Vg (spec table).  L_c(f) in tau_c and in V^R is
    the statement cost in the single-law theory {f} (D1, C1 for V^R).
C8  dec_c(EC): the 9 rules as lhs+rhs trees, pattern variables in a leaf scope of 3, rule operators
    d/Dt/Dq/Sm as Calls (library 4); C3 adds '->' (2 chars) per rule.  Charged only at D0/D1.
C9  C4: law-licensed corpus constants (N2 masses, HK spring constants, UG's G; g and ke always) are
    never lumped; they stay leaves and cost B once per corpus if referenced (the N2 decoder references
    the masses of every body).  Each system's fair min treats them as free; the corpus term is added
    once.  Per-system decomposition: each corpus constant's B bits are split equally among the systems
    referencing it.  Lumping: in an Add/Mul the args that are state-free and contain an unlicensed
    parameter (plus numeric factors) form one constant; constants are identified by sp.cancel.
C10 C4 + PC without N2-licensed masses: the PC decoder needs the N-1 mass ratios, charged as
    N-1 extra constants (B bits each, sign-known).
C11 C2: kind / arity / role frequencies Laplace-fitted on the C1-CSE'd all-explicit empty-theory
    representations (family P Newtonian, family G explicit); holes/formals use p(param).
C12 C3: characters of sp.sstr of the (unevaluated) tree; temps, output refs, constants and holes are
    printed with 2-char names; templates renamed to 2 chars (Fh1->F1, Fh2->F2, Vh1->H1, Vh2->H2,
    miner Zx); a definition pays name + '=' (3 chars).
C13 Miner: template definition = gamma(#holes+1) + body whose leaves are holes or global symbols
    (C1: log2(#holes + #global symbols)); C3 definition "Zx=" + text; C3 call = 2 + 2 + (#holes-1)
    chars.  System-local leaves (temps, output refs, local C4 constants) are never concrete template
    leaves (LGG maps them to holes).  The candidate pool is regenerated each iteration.  Accept guard:
    a template is kept only if the exact corpus length decreases (else the next candidate is tried).
    Earlier template bodies are not rewritten by later templates.  The miner operates on the
    miner-off chosen formulation of each system.
C14 C4 miner dictionary: groups of identical lumped constants in >= 2 systems, largest first, accepted
    if the exact length decreases; dictionary entries join every system's leaf scope.
C15 Track R: EC includes C = 0 (N^2 slots) and u = 0 (P04) as literally specified; r_fk is the 7.1
    proxy.  N3 (1D): symmetric K for wall systems (wall = grounded node), plus zero row sums for
    isolated systems.  Cells whose linearized ground truth violates the law's equations are excluded
    and reported.  P07 is reported only.  G06 uses the stable off-axis equilibrium (draws repeated
    until R*Om^2 > g).
C16 R2: sigma_slot = product of the scales of all parameter symbols in the slot's symbolic linearized
    entry (1 for structurally zero slots); eps = 2^-8.  R3: integer draws {1..4} for all parameters;
    log2 index = log2( sqrt(det(R_b R_b^T)) / prod(SNF invariant factors of R_b) ).  R4: exact
    conjugate-Gaussian evidence (Laplace is exact here), prior N(0,I), H_f prior = restriction to the
    subspace, accelerations observed with noise 1e-3 on 10 simulated trajectories (t in [0,10]).
C17 POS: Track G (C4) V_add = #sign-known constants under the empty theory - L(POS), V_loo = same under
    LAW9; sign-known iff the sign follows from m>0, k>0 (all masses incl. M0 and generalized masses,
    all spring constants); miner-on counts after the dictionary.  Track R: known masses, prior
    restricted to H_EC, fraction with K = -MA positive definite (1e5 samples); n/a if K is not PD at
    the truth (zero modes).
C18 K1-primary and K1-machine use the uncharged decoder; K1-precision is decided at D1 (other levels
    reported).  kappa = V+_mid/V_mid (-inf if V_mid <= 0); hypothesis (iii) uses the median kappa over
    the 4 primary codes.  If K2-collapse is INDETERMINATE it counts as not fired in K2-overall.
C19 U5 metric: normwise relative error max|a_rep - a_true| / max|a_true|.
C20 K2-deg-G law-level fit and the 7.3 regression include an intercept; M1 uses law fixed effects
    (no global intercept).  7.3 per-system L+ includes (C4) B x corpus constants referenced by that
    system.
C21 Optional miner-on Shapley: computed for D1 only, and only if the main run finished within 2 h.

Bug fixes / unfair encodings (in the main pipeline):
R1  No-N2 baseline (fairness): every Newtonian program without N2 (std, output-referencing, D2 gradient)
    is also offered in factored form (sum of forces)/m_i per body -- pure algebra with the base Mul and
    scalar*vector decoding -- as an extra candidate of the fair min (the per-term division form is kept).
R2  Side information of decoder laws: the N2 decoder (and PC's a_N = -sum m_i a_i/m_N) read the
    body -> mass-parameter assignment for free.  It is now paid: one parameter-leaf reference per body
    in the code at hand (C3: mass names + separators; C4: pointer to the licensed corpus constant; PC
    under C4 without licensed masses keeps its N-1 mass-ratio constants and needs no pointer).  EC's
    (q_i, v_i) pairing is NOT charged: every formulation of a second-order model (explicit, implicit,
    Newtonian outputs in body/coordinate order) presupposes the same state convention, so it is common
    to all theories and would cancel.
R3  Track R, LC: the specified rows (symmetric part of (M(x)J^T)A = 0) are the condition for a
    linearization about the origin.  About an equilibrium r0 != 0 (P12, P13) the first-order condition of
    dL/dt = 0 is w^T A = 0 with w_i = m_i J r0_i (N rows, A only, as the spec restricts LC to A); this is
    now used there.  P12 LC becomes valid (codim 6, residual ~1e-16); P13 stays excluded (gravity torque).
    R3 code: for P12 the rows contain sqrt(3); for integer theta a + b*sqrt(3) rows split into a and b, so
    the integer lattice codim is 12 (reported; the real codim 6 is used for R1/R2/R4).
R4  Law statements are coded with the same base mechanisms as models: sympy CSE ('basic') per statement
    item, then greedy inlining; temps see formals + earlier temps; #temps announced by
    Elias-gamma(#temps+1) (C3/C6: each definition pays name + '=').  This supersedes C7's "no CSE" and adds
    1 bit per statement item in C1/C2/C4.  The 9 decoder rewrite rules are unchanged: sympy CSE finds no
    shared subterm in any rule side (checked at run time, reported as dec_rules_cse_temps).
R5  Family-G gradient-residual forms (D2) no longer put z1..zn in the leaf scope (they never reference z).
R6  No-LC baseline (fairness): a central force term may be written s*V(dx,dy) as a separate summand in
    every theory (base Mul + V + scalar*vector decoding); Cn(s,dx,dy) abbreviates exactly this.  Added as a
    candidate of the per-term min (the merged explicit V(sum_x, sum_y) remains available).
R8  Symbol identity (replaces C1): only the identities the spec asserts are kept (mA, mB, mC of P10;
    kA, kB, kC; G, g, ke; and P03/P04 share body mA, spring kA and damper b).  Unnamed bodies get
    system-local 2-character mass symbols (P08 mK; P12 mD, mE, mF; P13 mG; P14 mH; P15 mP, mQ, mR), and
    P06's chain bodies (m6..m9) are no longer identified with P11's m1..m4.  truth.pkl was rebuilt.
    reps.sign_known recognises the new names.

Added computations, reported alongside (the spec's own statistics remain the decisive ones):

    Family G may write the residual Dt(Dq(Lt,v^)) - Dq(Lt,q^) once under Map with 2 bound variables in
    scope, as the EL decoder does implicitly.  (Family-P gradient forms do not get the map.)  Reported as
    a K1 machine variant and as a K2-collapse setting.  Two bugs caught by U5 in the first r1 run and fixed

R9  Track R EC_sym: EC restricted to its Hamiltonian-symmetry rows (MA symmetric only; no C = 0 / u = 0
